How it works

Three steps to dinner

01
📸
Snap your fridge

Take up to 4 photos of your fridge and 4 of your pantry. The more you show, the better the recipes.

02
🔍
AI scans ingredients

Our AI vision instantly identifies every ingredient it can see — produce, condiments, leftovers, all of it. Edit the list if anything's off.

03
🍽
Cook great recipes

Get 6 real, highly-rated recipes matched to what you have. Filter by cuisine or cook time. Full step-by-step instructions included.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

🤖
AI ingredient scanning

Advanced vision AI identifies ingredients from your photos with remarkable accuracy — including fresh produce, condiments, leftovers, and packaged goods.

⭐
Real, rated recipes

Recipes sourced from across the internet — Allrecipes, BBC Good Food, Food Network and more — with real ratings and source links so you know they're tried and tested.

🌍
Filter by cuisine & time

In the mood for Italian? Need dinner in under 30 minutes? Filter your recipe results instantly by cuisine type and cooking time.

🔖
Save to your profile

Create an account and save recipes you love. Build your personal cookbook from the dishes you've discovered and made.

📊
Ingredient match score

Each recipe shows exactly what percentage of ingredients you already have — and a clear list of the few things you'd need to pick up.

🗑
Reduce food waste

Use what's already in your fridge before it goes off. Fridge to Fork is the easiest way to stop throwing money in the bin.
